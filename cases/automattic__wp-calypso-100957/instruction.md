# Theme Previewer: Better contrast on the style variations

## Where

In many different onboarding, where we show the theme picker + theme previewer. Example: https://wordpress.com/setup/onboarding, after selecting goals and moving to design selection.

## Problem

Some of these color palettes have pretty bad contrast.

> **Visual material 1:** `/testbed/assets/asset_01.png`

## Solution

Designers should revise the suggested style variations and fix those who do not pass a bare minimum of contrast.
